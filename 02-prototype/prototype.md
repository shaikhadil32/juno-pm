## What it demonstrates

_One weekly backlog preparation flow: a RocketShip integrations PM reviews recurring customer problems, inspects the supporting Slack messages and support tickets, checks suggested Jira matches, and records a next step.

The prototype distinguishes source records, distinct reports, and identifiable customer accounts. It also keeps a potentially serious one-off report visible even though it does not qualify as recurring._

_____

## Debrief

- **What worked:**
_Evidence expansion, investigation decisions, simulated Jira linking, grouping corrections, and the empty-week scenario worked in browser checks. Removing two Cedar records from a group correctly reduced its counts from three accounts, eight records, and seven reports to two accounts, six records, and five reports._


- **What broke / felt like a toy:**
  _The groupings and Jira suggestions are precomputed, so the prototype does not demonstrate actual AI synthesis. Decisions disappear on refresh, and no changes reach Jira. The layout still needs refinement for smaller screens. PM usability and time savings have not yet been validated._

  
- **What I'd change next pass:**
_Improve readability with a default theme/group view and an alternative list view. Add filters for themes and sources, plus sorting by recurrence and distinct customer count. Include direct links to original sources and a “Run update” button showing when the review was last refreshed. In later iterations, add AI search across customer problems and evidence, along with share and download options for backlog discussions._
